AirPods Pro 2 provide Noise Cancellation, Transparency, and Adaptive Audio listening modes. Press and hold the stem of either AirPod to change between the enabled noise-control modes. The modes included in the gesture can be adjusted in AirPods settings on a connected iPhone or iPad. Noise Cancellation reduces external sound using outward- and inward-facing microphones. Transparency allows surrounding sound to pass through so the wearer can remain aware of the environment. Adaptive Audio combines active noise cancellation and transparency behavior and adjusts to changing conditions. Conversation Awareness can lower media volume and emphasize nearby voices when the wearer starts speaking. Noise-control availability and names can depend on the connected device software. A proper ear-tip seal improves both sound and noise-control performance. Users should not rely on noise cancellation to block important environmental warnings, especially in traffic or other safety-sensitive situations.
