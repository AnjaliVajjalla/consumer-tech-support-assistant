AirPods Pro 2 can appear in Find My after they are paired with an iPhone or iPad signed in to an Apple Account. Find My must be enabled on a paired device before the AirPods are lost. The Find My network can show supported AirPods even when they are not currently near the paired device; otherwise Find My may show the last known location or report that no location was found. In the Find My app, choose Devices and select the AirPods to view their current or last known location. AirPods Pro 2 support locating the individual earbuds and the charging case. A compatible iPhone may provide directional guidance to the precise location of the charging case. Users can also play a sound when supported, enable separation alerts, or mark the AirPods as lost. If the AirPods are offline or need charging, their current position may not be available until they reconnect.
