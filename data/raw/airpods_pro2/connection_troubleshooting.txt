# AirPods Connection Troubleshooting Steps

1. Update your device to the latest iOS, iPadOS, or macOS version.

2. Charge your AirPods by placing both in the charging case and verifying they're charging properly.

3. Enable Bluetooth through Settings on your iPhone/iPad or System Settings on Mac.

4. Check Control Center and tap the AirPlay button to see if your AirPods appear in the device list. If they do, select them and test with audio.

5. Reset the case by closing the lid, waiting 15 seconds, then opening it again.

6. Enter pairing mode by either:
   - Double-tapping the front of AirPods 4 or Pro 3 cases until the status light flashes white, OR
   - Pressing and holding the setup button on other models' cases until the status light flashes white.

7. Position the case next to your device with the lid open.

8. Follow on-screen instructions to complete the pairing process.

9. Reset your AirPods if connection still fails by accessing the reset instructions.
