# AirPods Pro 2 with MagSafe Charging Case (USB-C) - Tech Specs

Year Introduced: 2023

Audio Technology
The device features a custom high-excursion Apple driver and Pro-level Active Noise Cancellation alongside adaptive audio, transparency mode, and personalized spatial audio with head tracking capabilities.

Hearing Health
Includes hearing test and hearing aid features (FDA authorized), plus hearing protection functionality.

Sensors & Chip
Equipped with dual beamforming microphones, skin-detect sensor, and the Apple H2 headphone chip in earbuds, plus U1 chip in the charging case.

Controls
Touch controls enable media playback, call management, and volume adjustment. Voice activation works with Siri commands.

Durability
IP54 rated for dust, sweat, and water resistance across both earbuds and case.

Dimensions & Weight
Each earbud measures approximately 30.9mm height and weighs 5.3 grams. The charging case is 45.2mm tall, 60.6mm wide, and weighs 50.8 grams.

Battery Life
Up to 6 hours per charge on earbuds; total of 30 hours with case included.

Connectivity
Bluetooth 5.3 wireless technology enables device pairing.

Environmental Features
Incorporates recycled materials and meets energy efficiency standards.
