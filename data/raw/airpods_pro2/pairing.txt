To pair AirPods Pro 2 with an iPhone or iPad, turn on Bluetooth, keep both earbuds in the charging case, open the lid, and hold the case near the device until the setup animation appears. Follow the onscreen instructions and tap Done. Once paired to one Apple device, AirPods can connect automatically to other supported Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account. For manual pairing with a Mac or another Bluetooth device, open the case with the AirPods inside and press and hold the setup button on the back for about five seconds, until the status light flashes white. Then select the AirPods from the device's Bluetooth list. Keep the case open during manual pairing. If the setup animation does not appear, confirm Bluetooth is on, charge the AirPods and case, and use the manual pairing steps. AirPods Pro 2 use the rear setup button, unlike newer models that may use a different case gesture.
