AirPods Pro 2 charge whenever they are placed in their case and the lid is closed. The USB-C version of the MagSafe Charging Case can be charged with a USB-C cable and compatible power adapter. The case can also charge on a MagSafe charger, a Qi-certified wireless charger, or a compatible Apple Watch charger. For wireless charging, place the closed case with its status light facing up and center it on the charger. The light appears briefly when alignment is correct, then turns off while charging continues. When the earbuds are in the open case, the status light represents their charge. When the earbuds are out, it represents the case. Green means fully charged, while amber means less than one full charge remains. Battery percentages can also be checked in the AirPods settings on an iPhone or iPad or through the Bluetooth menu on a Mac. Optimized Battery Charging can reduce time spent fully charged to help limit battery wear.
