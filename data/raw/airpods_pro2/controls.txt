AirPods Pro 2 use a force sensor on each stem. Press the stem once to play or pause audio, or to answer a call. Press twice to skip forward, and press three times to move to the previous track. Press and hold to switch among configured noise-control modes. Swipe up or down on the stem to adjust volume. Controls work on either earbud, although settings can assign different press-and-hold actions to the left and right sides. Siri can also handle playback, volume, calls, and other supported actions through voice commands. Automatic Ear Detection can pause audio when an AirPod is removed and resume when it is replaced. Some actions depend on the connected device and software version. Control settings are available by opening AirPods settings on a connected iPhone or iPad. The charging case controls pairing and status but does not provide the same media-control gestures as the earbud stems.
