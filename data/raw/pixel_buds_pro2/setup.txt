For initial Pixel Buds Pro 2 setup, keep both earbuds in the charging case and open the lid. Unlock the Android phone and make sure Bluetooth is enabled. Press and hold the pairing button on the case for about three seconds, until the light begins its white pairing animation. Tap the Pixel Buds pairing notification on the phone and follow the onscreen instructions. Android 6.0 or later is required for the documented Android setup flow. To connect to a computer, open the case with the earbuds inside, enable Bluetooth on the computer, hold the pairing button until the light indicates pairing, and select Google Pixel Buds from the available devices. Google also supports setup and management on ChromeOS, macOS, and Windows through its Pixel Buds web companion experience. If the buds do not appear, charge the case and earbuds, close and reopen the lid, and restart pairing mode.
