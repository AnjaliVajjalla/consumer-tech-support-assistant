Pixel Buds Pro 2 offer Noise Cancellation, Transparency, Adaptive, and Off noise-control modes. Noise Cancellation reduces outside sound. Transparency lets surrounding sound through. Adaptive mode keeps the wearer aware while reducing loud or unwanted noise and uses the Tensor A1 chip to adjust processing. Press and hold an earbud to switch among configured modes, or choose a mode in Pixel Buds settings. Noise controls can still be used when wearing only one earbud. Conversation Detection can temporarily switch from Noise Cancellation to Transparency and pause media when the wearer begins speaking, then resume media afterward. The press-and-hold gesture is customizable, so it may control the digital assistant instead of noise control. Fit and ear-tip seal influence sound and cancellation performance. In noisy calling environments, Google notes that Transparency mode can interfere with call clarity, so another noise-control mode may work better.
