Pixel Buds Pro 2 support gestures on either earbud. Tap once to play or pause media or answer a call. Double-tap to move to the next track, end or reject a call, or stop the digital assistant. Triple-tap returns to the previous track. Swipe forward to raise volume and backward to lower it. Press and hold can switch active noise-control modes or invoke the configured digital assistant. The press-and-hold behavior can be customized in Pixel Buds settings. Pixel Buds Pro 2 also support head gestures: a nod can answer a call, while a head shake can reject a call. Touch controls can be disabled if accidental activation is a problem. Supported gestures and assistant behavior depend on the connected device, software version, and configured settings. If gestures stop working, confirm the earbuds are connected, clean and dry the touch surfaces, and check that touch controls remain enabled.
