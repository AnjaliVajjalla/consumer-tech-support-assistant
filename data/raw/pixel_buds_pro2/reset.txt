Resetting Pixel Buds Pro 2 removes settings, the device name, feature preferences, and Find My ownership information. First forget the Pixel Buds in the connected device's Bluetooth settings. Connect the charging case to power with a USB-C cable. Place both earbuds correctly in the case and leave the lid open. Press and hold the pairing button on the back of the case for 30 seconds. Release it when the earbud status light stops its animation and turns off. During reset, the light blinks orange and white. When the light returns to a bouncing white pairing animation, the reset is complete and the earbuds are ready to pair again. A reset is required for previously owned or refurbished Pixel Buds Pro 2 so ownership can transfer and Find My tracking can be configured for the new owner. If the process does not begin, reseat both earbuds in the case and keep the case connected to power.
