For missing or poor Pixel Buds Pro 2 audio, first clean the speaker and microphone meshes gently and let the earbuds dry completely before use. Confirm the earbuds and case are charged and that current firmware is installed. Check that Bluetooth is on, the buds are within range, and both are paired. Turn Bluetooth off briefly and back on. If automatic reconnection fails, connect from Bluetooth settings. For persistent problems, forget the buds, restart the device, and pair them again. Audio skips can also be addressed by placing the buds in the case for one minute, reducing obstacles, checking the internet connection for streamed media, and resetting the buds. Low bass may result from a poor seal, incorrect ear-tip size, dirt, or equalizer settings. During calls in noisy environments, try a different active noise-control mode because Transparency can sometimes interfere with call quality. Do not insert objects into microphone or speaker openings.
