Pixel Buds Pro 2 use Bluetooth 5.4 and custom 11 mm dynamic drivers. Audio features include Active Noise Cancellation with Silent Seal 2.0, Transparency mode, pressure relief, and Conversation Detection. Each earbud has three microphones, capacitive touch sensors, an infrared proximity sensor, an accelerometer, and a gyroscope. The case has a speaker for Find My Device, USB-C charging, and Qi-certified wireless charging. The earbuds carry an IP54 water-protection rating, while the case is rated IPX4. With Active Noise Cancellation on, Google lists up to 8 hours from the earbuds and up to 30 hours with the charging case. With ANC off, the corresponding figures are up to 12 and 48 hours. A five-minute case charge can provide up to one hour of listening with ANC on. Each earbud weighs about 4.7 grams with the medium tip, and the case with earbuds weighs about 65 grams.
