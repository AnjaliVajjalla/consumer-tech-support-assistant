The WH-1000XM5 turns noise canceling on automatically the first time it is powered on after purchase or initialization. Press the NC/AMB button to switch between Noise Canceling and Ambient Sound modes. The exact cycle can be customized in the Sony Sound Connect app, including whether an off position appears. Noise canceling reduces surrounding sound by producing an opposing signal and is most effective against lower-frequency sounds such as vehicle or air-conditioner noise. It does not eliminate every sound. Performance can change with fit, wearing conditions, and the environment because Auto NC Optimizer adjusts operation automatically. Do not cover the microphones on the earcups, because that can weaken noise canceling or cause feedback. A poor fit can also reduce the effect. Noise canceling can continue when the supplied headphone cable is connected, provided the headset remains powered on. Ambient Sound mode deliberately passes outside sound through the microphones so the wearer can remain aware of the environment.
