The Sony WH-1000XM5 weighs approximately 250 grams and uses a built-in lithium-ion battery. Charging from empty takes about three and a half hours under specified conditions. The supported operating temperature is 0 to 40 degrees Celsius, and the supported charging temperature is 5 to 35 degrees Celsius. Bluetooth communication uses version 5.2, Power Class 1, with a stated line-of-sight maximum range of about 10 meters or 30 feet. Real range varies with obstacles, interference, reception, antennas, and the connected device. Supported Bluetooth profiles include A2DP, AVRCP, HFP, and HSP. Supported audio codecs include SBC, AAC, and LDAC. With the headset powered on and connected by cable, frequency response is listed as 4 Hz to 40,000 Hz. The USB charging input supports standard 5 V power and compatible USB Power Delivery configurations. Specifications can change, so the official Sony Help Guide should be treated as the current authority.
