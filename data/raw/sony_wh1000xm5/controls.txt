The WH-1000XM5 has a touch sensor control panel on the right earcup. Double-tap the panel to play or pause audio. Swipe forward to move to the next track and backward to return to the beginning of the current or previous track. Swipe up to increase volume and down to decrease it. The left earcup contains the power button and the NC/AMB button for noise canceling and Ambient Sound mode. A tactile dot on the left unit helps identify orientation. The USB Type-C port and orange charging indicator are also on the left unit, along with the headphone-cable input. Built-in microphones support noise canceling and voice pickup. The headset includes wearing detection; removing it can pause playback and affect available touch operations. Touch gestures may depend on whether the connected Bluetooth device supports the required remote-control profile. If touch control behaves unexpectedly, check that the headset is worn correctly and that the sensor panel is clean and dry.
