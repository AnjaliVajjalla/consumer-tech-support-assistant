# WH-1000XM5 Bluetooth Pairing Instructions

## Prerequisites
Before pairing, ensure:
- The Bluetooth device is within 1 meter (3 feet) of the headset
- The headset has sufficient charge
- You have the Bluetooth device's operating instructions available

## Pairing Steps

Step 1: Enter Pairing Mode
For initial pairing after purchase or reset, simply power on the headset, it automatically enters pairing mode. For subsequent devices, press and hold the power button for about 5 seconds. The blue indicator will flash twice repeatedly, and you'll hear "Pairing" as voice guidance.

Step 2: Search on Bluetooth Device
Access the pairing procedure on your Bluetooth device and search for available headsets. "WH-1000XM5" will be displayed on the list of detected devices.

Step 3: Select and Pair
Choose the WH-1000XM5 from the list. If a passkey is requested, enter "0000."

Step 4: Establish Connection
Complete the Bluetooth connection process. Some devices connect automatically after pairing completes. You'll receive a notification sound confirming successful connection.

## Key Notes
- Pairing mode cancels automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity
- The headset supports up to 8 paired devices simultaneously
- Once paired, devices don't require re-pairing except after initialization or deletion
