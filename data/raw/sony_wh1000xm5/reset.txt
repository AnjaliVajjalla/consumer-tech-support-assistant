Reset the WH-1000XM5 if it cannot be turned on or operated normally. Connect the headset to an AC outlet using the supplied USB Type-C cable and a compatible USB AC adapter. While it is connected to power, press the power button and the NC/AMB button at the same time. This reboots the headset controls. A reset retains Bluetooth pairing information and other saved settings, so previously paired devices should remain registered. Resetting is different from initializing the headset. Initialization restores factory settings and deletes all Bluetooth pairing information. If the headset still does not operate correctly after a reset, Sony directs the user to initialize it and pair devices again. Do not confuse this procedure with simply turning the headset off and on. Make sure the headset is connected to power before pressing the two buttons together. If problems continue after reset and initialization, use official Sony support rather than attempting hardware repair.
