The WH-1000XM5 uses a built-in rechargeable lithium-ion battery. Charge it with the supplied USB Type-C cable and a compatible USB AC adapter, or connect it to a running computer. The orange charging indicator lights while charging and turns off when charging finishes. A full charge from empty takes about three and a half hours, although conditions can change the time. Sony recommends an adapter capable of at least 1.5 A; a USB Power Delivery adapter can shorten charging time. The headset turns off automatically if charging begins while it is on. Bluetooth connection, playback, pairing, and other headset operations are unavailable during charging. Charging may fail with a cable other than the supplied cable or with some adapters. Disconnect the USB cable after charging finishes. Keep the charging temperature between 5 and 35 degrees Celsius. If the indicator repeatedly flashes slowly in orange, the charging environment may be outside the supported temperature range.
