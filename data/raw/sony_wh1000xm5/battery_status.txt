Press the power button while the WH-1000XM5 is on to hear voice guidance for the approximate remaining battery charge. The headset may announce that the battery is fully charged or report an approximate percentage. The spoken value is an estimate and can differ from the actual remaining charge. The Sony Sound Connect app can also display the remaining battery level. When the remaining charge reaches about 10 percent or lower, the orange indicator flashes for roughly 15 seconds after the headset is turned on. A warning beep and a Low battery voice message indicate that the headset should be charged soon. When the battery is exhausted, the headset gives a warning, announces that the battery is empty, and turns off automatically. Battery information may also appear on some connected smartphones, but the exact display depends on the device and operating system. If the reported level seems incorrect, fully charge the headset and check again.
