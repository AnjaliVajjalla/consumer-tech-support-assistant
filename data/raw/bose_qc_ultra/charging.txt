Turn the QuietComfort Ultra Headphones off and remove them from your head before charging. Connect the small end of the USB cable to the USB-C port on the left earcup and connect the other end to a USB-A power source such as a computer or wall charger. The status light glows solid amber during charging and solid white when charging is complete. A full charge can take up to three hours. Bose lists up to 24 hours of playback with Immersive Audio off and up to 18 hours with it on. A 15-minute charge can provide up to two and a half hours with Immersive Audio off or up to two hours with it on. A voice prompt announces battery level when the headphones turn on. Bluetooth operation is disabled while charging. Built-in battery protection stops active charging when full, so leaving the cable connected does not continuously overcharge the battery.
