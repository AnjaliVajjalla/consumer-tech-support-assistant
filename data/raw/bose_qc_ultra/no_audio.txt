If the QuietComfort Ultra Headphones connect but produce no audio, disconnect any charging cable because Bluetooth audio is disabled during charging. Restart the source device and try another app to determine whether the problem is application-specific. On Windows, choose the Bluetooth entry with the headphone or speaker icon rather than the LE entry. Remove old Bose entries and pair again if necessary. Test with another Bluetooth device to separate a headphone problem from a source-device problem. For wired listening, connect the supplied audio cable securely and keep the headphones powered on; their active equalization requires power even with the analog cable. Connecting the audio cable disables Bluetooth. USB is for charging and does not provide USB audio on this model. Inspect the audio cable for damage and bypass adapters or extension cables while troubleshooting. If needed, reboot the headphones using Bose's documented USB-and-button procedure, which restarts controls without erasing settings.
