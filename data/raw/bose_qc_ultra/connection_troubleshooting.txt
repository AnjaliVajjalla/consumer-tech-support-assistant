If the QuietComfort Ultra Headphones repeatedly disconnect, first confirm they are not charging because Bluetooth is disabled during charging. Keep the headphones within about 10 meters or 33 feet of the source and reduce walls, doors, or nearby electronics that may interfere. Restart the phone, computer, or tablet. Remove the headphones from the source device's Bluetooth memory, including duplicate LE listings, then pair them again using the normal headphone listing. Clearing the headphones' stored Bluetooth device list can also resolve corrupted connection history. Test with a different source device to determine whether the issue is specific to the original device. For a non-destructive reboot, disconnect audio and USB cables, hold the Power or Bluetooth button, connect USB power while continuing to hold, wait two seconds, then release. This reboot does not erase product settings. Windows users can also check whether power management is turning off the computer's Bluetooth adapter.
