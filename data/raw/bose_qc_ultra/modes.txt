Quiet mode provides full noise cancellation with standard stereo audio. Aware mode provides transparency so surrounding sound can be heard. Immersion mode combines full noise cancellation with Immersive Audio. Custom modes can combine a chosen noise-control level, wind blocking, and Immersive Audio settings. Up to seven custom modes can be created in the Bose app. Quiet, Aware, and Immersion are favorites by default. To cycle through favorite modes from the headphones, press and hold the multi-function button and release it when the desired mode is announced. Modes can also be selected in the Bose app. ActiveSense is available within Aware mode and can temporarily increase noise cancellation when sudden or loud sound occurs, then reduce it again when the sound ends. Wind Block is available through a custom mode and adjusts noise cancellation for windy conditions. By default, the headphones power on in Quiet mode unless the Bose app is configured to restore the last-used mode.
