Put the Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones into pairing mode by turning them on and holding the Bluetooth or Power button until the status light blinks blue and the discoverable voice prompt plays. Enable Bluetooth on the phone, tablet, or computer and select the headphone name from the available-device list. A connection tone confirms success. The Bose app can also start pairing by opening the product's source or Bluetooth controls and choosing to add a new device. On Windows, the product can appear more than once. Choose the listing with a headphone or speaker icon, not a listing prefixed with LE. Keep the headphones close to the source device during setup. If they do not appear, refresh the Bluetooth list, remove an old Bose listing, and restart pairing mode. Bluetooth functions are disabled while the headphones are charging, so disconnect the USB charging cable before attempting wireless pairing.
